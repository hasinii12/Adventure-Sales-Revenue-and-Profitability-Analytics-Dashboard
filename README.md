# 📊 Adventure Sales, Revenue & Profitability Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project showcases an interactive Power BI dashboard built using Excel-based sales data to analyze sales performance, revenue trends, profitability, inventory, and customer loyalty across multiple countries and products.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key business metrics, identify top-performing products and regions, and track trends over time using dynamic filters and KPIs.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze sales and revenue performance across countries
- Identify high-performing and low-performing products
- Track profit margins and business growth over time
- Provide actionable insights through interactive visualizations

---

## 🔍 Key Features
- KPIs: Total Stock, Quantity Purchased, Median Sales, Net Revenue, Profit Margin
- Country-wise Analysis: Loyalty points, median sales, yearly profit margins
- Product-wise Analysis: Quantity sold and net revenue contribution
- Time-Series Analysis: Median sales and profit margin trends over time
- Interactive Filters: Date range and country slicers

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights
- Loyalty Points by Country  
- Quantity Sold by Product  
- Median Sales by Country  
- Median Sales Over Time  
- Net Revenue by Product  
- Year-to-Date Profit Margin  
- Yearly Profit Margin by Country
